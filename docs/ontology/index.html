Description

  • Vocabulary for patent license clearance. A Grant relates a patent, a licensee, a field of use, a territory, a term, and an exclusivity type. Every fact is bitemporal and carries provenance. Fields of use and territories are SKOS concepts, and a broader concept means wider rights coverage.

  • Under the open world assumption, a missing fact means unknown. The rule engine, not the reasoner, turns unknown into the verdict NO DATA.
